About Study Planner

Study Planner is a website that helps students keep track of their classes and assessments across the college year, all in one place.

When you have several classes, each with its own deadlines, it is easy to lose track of what is due and when. Study Planner puts every class and every assessment in one planner, shows how far through each piece of work you are, and marks every due date on a calendar.

What you can do

Organise by semester

Add your classes to semester 1 or semester 2, and rename or remove them as your timetable changes.

Track assessments

Add each assessment with its due date. They are listed in date order, so the next deadline is always at the top.

See your progress

Move the slider as you work on an assessment, and the progress bar shows how close you are to finishing.

Calendar view

Every assessment appears on the calendar on its due date, so you can see your busy weeks coming.

Your own account

Sign up to keep your planner saved. Your work is private: each student only ever sees their own classes and assessments.

Try it first

The home page has a demo with example classes, so you can try everything out before signing up.

How it works

  1. Sign up for a free account.
  2. Pick a semester and add the classes you are taking.
  3. Choose a class and add its assessments with their due dates.
  4. Update your progress as you go, and check the calendar to plan your weeks.

Who made it

Study Planner was made by a group of first-year students as a Team Working project at Dundee and Angus College. It is built with HTML, CSS and JavaScript, with a Python (Flask) server and an SQLite database to save each student's planner.
